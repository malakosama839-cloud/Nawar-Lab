Chapter 4: Alternating Current (AC) Circuits

How resistors, coils and capacitors behave differently when the current keeps changing direction — and how a coil and a capacitor can sing together in resonance.

Effective value Vrms = Vm/√2
Inductive reactance XL = 2πfL
Capacitive reactance XC = 1/(2πfC)
Resonance fr = 1/(2π√(LC))

In DC, a resistor is the only thing that opposes current. In AC the story changes completely: a coil fights any change of current, a capacitor blocks steady current but happily passes rapidly changing current, and the voltages across the components no longer rise and fall together. To handle this we use phasors (rotating arrows), reactance and impedance — and when XL exactly cancels XC we get resonance, the principle behind every radio tuner.
