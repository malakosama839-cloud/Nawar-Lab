The Oscillatory Circuit & Resonance

Energy sloshing between a capacitor and a coil — the electrical heart of every radio.

The Oscillatory (Tank) Circuit

Connect a capacitor C to a coil L (with negligible resistance) and the charge starts to slosh back and forth between them:

  • The charged capacitor discharges through the coil — the current grows and energy is stored in the coil's magnetic field.
  • When the capacitor is empty, the current does not stop: the coil's back-EMF keeps it flowing, and it recharges the capacitor with the opposite polarity.
  • Then everything runs in reverse. Energy swaps between the capacitor's electric field and the coil's magnetic field — like water sloshing from end to end of a tub.

This produces an alternating current whose frequency depends only on L and C — it is the circuit's own natural (resonance) frequency:

fr = 1 / (2π √(L × C))bigger L or bigger C → slower energy swaps → lower frequency

Resonance

In a series RLC circuit, resonance happens at the frequency where the two reactances are exactly equal:

XL = XC  ⇒  Z = R (minimum)  ,  I = V/R (maximum)  ,  φ = 0the reactances cancel — the circuit behaves as pure resistance and V, I are in phase

Since Z drops to its minimum, the current rises to a sharp maximum at fr and falls away on either side. The circuit "picks out" one frequency and ignores the rest — exactly what a tuner must do.

Tuning a Radio

Every broadcasting station sends out waves of its own frequency. A receiver's LC circuit is driven by all of them at once, but only the station whose frequency equals fr produces a large resonant current. You "tune" the radio by turning the knob = changing the capacitance C until the circuit resonates at the wanted station. That's why the tuning dial changes stations, not volume.

Worked example 1: An LC circuit has L = 0.1 H and C = 25 μF. Find its resonance frequency.
fr = 1 / (2π√(LC)) = 1 / (2π × √(0.1 × 25×10⁻⁶)) = 1 / (2π × 1.581×10⁻³) ≈ 101 Hz.

Worked example 2 (radio tuning): A receiver coil has L = 0.1 mH. What capacitance tunes the circuit to a station at f = 1000 kHz?
C = 1 / (4π²f²L) = 1 / (4 × 9.87 × (10⁶)² × 10⁻⁴) = 1 / (3.948×10⁹) ≈ 2.53×10⁻¹⁰ F ≈ 253 pF.
