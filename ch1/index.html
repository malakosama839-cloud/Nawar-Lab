Chapter 1: Electric Current, Ohm's Law & Kirchhoff's Laws

The foundation of DC circuits: from the moving charge itself up to solving full circuits — with a live simulator on every topic.

By the end of this chapter you can…

  • Define current, potential difference and resistance, and state the direction of conventional vs actual current.
  • Use Ohm's law (V = IR) and compute resistance from a wire's length, area and resistivity.
  • Find the equivalent resistance of series and parallel networks.
  • Apply Ohm's law to a closed circuit: EMF, internal resistance, V = VB − I·r.
  • Calculate electrical power and energy consumption.
  • Apply Kirchhoff's first law (KCL) and second law (KVL) to circuit loops and junctions.
