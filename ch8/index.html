Chapter 8: Modern Electronics

Semiconductors, the diode, the transistor and logic gates — the physics inside every phone, computer and TV. Five short topics take you from a pure silicon crystal all the way to the gates that compute.

Topic 1 ⚡

Energy Bands & Semiconductors

Valence band, conduction band, the forbidden gap — and how doping creates n-type and p-type crystals.

 Topic 2 🚦

The p-n Junction (Diode)

The one-way valve: forward and reverse bias, the 0.7 V barrier and half-wave rectification.

 Topic 3 🎛️

The Transistor

Emitter, base, collector — and how a tiny base current (Ic = β·Ib) works as an amplifier and a switch.

 Topic 4 💾

Digital Signals & Binary

Analog vs digital, why digital beats noise, and converting decimal ↔ binary by powers of 2.

 Topic 5 🔀

Logic Gates

AND, OR and NOT — their symbols, truth tables and how combinations of gates build every circuit.

By the end of this chapter you can…

  • Compare conductors, insulators and semiconductors using the energy-band diagram and the size of the forbidden gap.
  • Explain intrinsic semiconductors (ne = nh) and identify majority carriers in n-type and p-type crystals.
  • Describe the depletion region and barrier potential of a p-n junction, and predict diode behaviour in forward and reverse bias.
  • Explain half-wave rectification and the role of the 4-diode bridge.
  • Use β = Ic/Ib and Ie = Ib + Ic in calculations, and describe the transistor as amplifier and switch.
  • Convert decimal ↔ binary and write the truth tables of AND, OR and NOT.
